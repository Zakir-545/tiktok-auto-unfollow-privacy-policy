Section 01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Auto Unfollow, also called FollowPilot (“the Extension”), handles your information. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of the Extension.

The Extension helps you unfollow accounts on TikTok, such as non-followers, mutuals or a list you pick, from your own signed-in TikTok tab. It works inside your browser. We never receive your TikTok data, and we never sell it.

Section 02Information we access

  • Your Following list: the accounts you follow and their public profile details, such as username, name, photo, follower count, video count and whether an account is verified or private, so the Radar can show them.
  • Follow-back status: whether each account follows you back, so non-followers and mutuals can be shown.
  • Your TikTok session: the Extension works in your open TikTok tab, where you are already signed in. It never asks for, reads or stores your password.
  • License key: if you buy Premium, the key you enter is used only to confirm your purchase.

Section 03How we use information

  • To show your list in the Radar with search, filters and insights.
  • To unfollow only the accounts you choose, in Turbo, Autopilot or Manual mode, while always skipping your Protected list and, if you choose, verified accounts and large creators.
  • To pace runs safely with breaks, a stop limit and an automatic cool-down.
  • To show live progress, keep your unfollow history and let you follow an account back.
  • To create the files you export and read the lists you import.

All of this happens on your own device, in your browser.

Section 04Where your data is kept

Your settings, Protected list, unfollow history, run status, trial count and license key are saved only in your browser on this device. Exported files are saved on your device, and imported lists are read only when you choose them.

Data saved by the Extension is deleted when you uninstall it.

Section 05What we share

We do not share your data with anyone, with one exception: checking your license. When you activate Premium, and when you open the Extension after that, your license key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad, our payment provider, only to confirm that your license is valid. No TikTok data is included.

Bug reports: the bug button opens Gmail with a ready-made message, including basic technical details such as the extension version, your settings and list counts. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

Section 06Our commitments

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never use your data for advertising or credit decisions.
  • We never use your data for anything except managing who you follow on TikTok.
  • We never add tracking, analytics or code from outside the Extension.

Section 07Children

The Extension is not intended for children.
